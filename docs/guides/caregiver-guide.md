## 1. Welcome

Welcome to the Ambitious about Autism training platform. If you are here, you are taking a meaningful step towards deepening your understanding of autism and how to support the people in your care.

This platform has been designed with busy practitioners in mind. You can work through training at your own pace, return to lessons whenever you need a refresher, and access resources and workshops arranged by your organisation — all in one place.

This guide explains how to use each part of the platform. You do not need any technical knowledge to get started. Take your time, and go at the pace that suits you.

## 2. Getting Started

### Your account

There are two ways to get an account:

1. **Created by your organisation's administrator.** You receive an email or a printable QR card with your credentials. If your admin set a temporary password, you will be asked to change it on your first sign-in.
2. **You register yourself.** Visit `/register`, choose **I work for or study at an existing organisation**, fill in your name, email, and pick your role. You will then receive a welcome email with a link to choose a password and sign in — no password to make up at the registration form itself. The link expires in 24 hours.

If you have not received the welcome email after self-registering, check your spam folder first. If it still hasn't arrived, ask your organisation's administrator.

### Logging in

Visit https://asd-training-app-v2.vercel.app and enter your email address. The login page detects how you should sign in based on your email:

**Email and password (most common)**
Enter your password and click **Sign in**. If this is your first login after self-registration or an admin reset, you may be prompted to change your password.

**Single Sign-On (SSO)**
If your organisation uses corporate SSO, a **Sign in with [your provider]** button appears after you type your email. Click it and you will be taken through your organisation's normal sign-in process.

**Google or Microsoft**
If the charity has enabled Google or Microsoft sign-in, those buttons also appear inline. You can use them whether or not your organisation has its own SSO.

### Forgotten your password?

Click **Forgot password** below the password field. Enter your email address and a password-reset link will be sent to your inbox. The link expires after one hour, so use it promptly. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder and contact your admin if it still does not arrive.

### Staying signed in

Your session lasts for 8 hours. After that you will be asked to sign in again. This is a security measure — particularly important on shared devices.

## 3. Your Dashboard

When you sign in, you land on your **Dashboard**. This is your home base for everything on the platform.

### Announcements

If your organisation or the platform administrators have posted any notices — new training content, upcoming events, reminders — they appear at the top of your dashboard. These are tailored to your organisation, so they are worth checking regularly.

### Your progress at a glance

The dashboard shows summary cards that give you a quick read on where you are:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Lessons completed | How many lessons you have finished across all your assigned training |
| Training progress | A breakdown of progress per training programme, with links to continue |

### Training progress panel

Below the summary cards, a more detailed panel shows each training programme assigned to you — for example "ASD Awareness Training" — with a progress bar and a button to continue or start each one.

### Upcoming Sessions card

If your organisation has any workshops or virtual sessions scheduled that you have been invited to, an **Upcoming Sessions** card will appear here showing the next session's title, date, and time.

## 4. Training Modules

The training content on this platform is organised into **programmes**, each made up of **modules**, which in turn contain **lessons**. Your organisation decides which programmes you are enrolled on.

### Finding your training

Your assigned training programmes are listed in the left-hand sidebar, for example "ASD Awareness Training". Click a programme name to open it.

### How programmes and modules work

Inside a programme, you will see a list of modules. Each module card shows:

- The module title and a short description
- How many lessons it contains and how many you have completed
- A **Start** or **Continue** button

You can access any lesson in any order — there is no requirement to unlock modules in sequence. If you want to revisit an earlier lesson or jump to a topic you need right now, you can do that freely.

### Working through a lesson

Click a module card to see its lessons, then click any lesson to open it. Lessons come in three types:

- **Text lessons** — structured reading with written content, interactive elements, and a quiz at the end.
- **Video lessons** — an embedded video with a transcript you can read alongside it, plus a quiz.
- **SCORM lessons** — packaged interactive content from specialist publishers, which plays directly in the browser.

### Progress tracking

Your progress is saved automatically as you complete lessons. If you close the browser mid-lesson, any lessons you have already finished will still be marked as complete. You can pick up where you left off at any time.

## 5. Lesson Features

Each lesson includes several features designed to make the learning experience accessible and engaging.

### Text-to-Speech (TTS)

A **Play** button appears on each lesson so you can listen to the lesson content read aloud. The voice is a British female voice called Lily. This is useful if you prefer to listen while doing something else, or if reading from a screen is tiring.

For lessons with interactive carousels, each slide has its own play button so you can listen slide by slide.

For text-only lessons, the play button appears at the bottom of the page.

### Interactive elements

Some lessons include interactive blocks embedded in the content:

- **Carousels** — swipeable or clickable slides, each with optional audio playback.
- **Hotspot images** — images with highlighted areas you can click to reveal more information.
- **Video embeds** — short clips embedded within the lesson text, separate from the main lesson video.
- **Rich text blocks** — formatted text, images, and other content woven throughout the lesson.

### Quiz questions

Most lessons end with a short quiz to check your understanding. Work through each question and submit your answers. There is no pass or fail threshold — the quiz is there to reinforce the learning, not to assess you.

### Certificate of Completion

After you complete a lesson (including the quiz, if there is one), a **Certificate of Completion** screen is displayed with the Ambitious about Autism logo. This confirms the lesson is marked as done on your record.

### Resources

Some lessons have downloadable resources attached — for example, PDF reference sheets or supporting documents. If a lesson has any, they appear in a **Resources** section below the lesson content as download links. Click the file name to download it.

## 6. Document Library

Your organisation may have a document library set up for you. This is a collection of documents — guides, policies, reference materials — that your organisation wants to make available to practitioners.

### Accessing the library

If collections have been shared with your role, they appear as individual links in the left-hand sidebar. You can also browse all collections you have access to by visiting `/library` directly.

### Viewing and downloading documents

Click a collection to open it and see the documents inside. Click any document to view it or download it. Your organisation's administrators can see how documents are being accessed, so they can ensure the most useful materials are always available.

## 7. Workshops and Sessions

Your organisation may invite you to virtual workshops or training sessions, run over Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or another platform.

### Upcoming sessions on your dashboard

When you have been invited to a session, an **Upcoming Sessions** card appears on your dashboard with the title, date, and time of the next one.

### Viewing all your sessions

Click **Workshops** in the left-hand sidebar to see the full list of sessions you have been invited to, along with their status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scheduled | The session is coming up — check the date and time |
| In Progress | The session is live right now |
| Completed | The session has finished |
| Cancelled | The session was cancelled by the organiser |

### Joining a session

When a session is live, a **Join** button appears next to it. Click it to open the meeting link and join directly.

### Recordings

After a completed session, the host or organiser may add a recording link. If one is available, a **Watch recording** link will appear on the session in your list. Click it to watch back at a time that suits you.

### If you are a session host

If your organisation's administrator has assigned you as a **host** for a session, you will see extra options when you open that session:

- **Add or update the meeting link** — paste the Zoom or Teams link that attendees will use to join.
- **Mark attendance** — check off which attendees were present once the session is underway or finished.
- **Add a recording URL** — paste a link to the recording after the session ends.

## 8. Settings

The **Settings** option is at the bottom of the left-hand sidebar, above Sign Out.

### Changing your password

Go to **Settings** to change your password at any time. You will need to enter your current password before setting a new one. Choose something secure that you will remember.

If you are prompted to change your password immediately after logging in, this means your administrator set a temporary password for you. You must change it before you can access the rest of the platform.

### Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA is not required for the Practitioner role. However, if your organisation's administrator has set up additional security requirements, you may be prompted to set up an authenticator app (TOTP). If this happens, the platform will guide you through the steps — you will need an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your phone.

### Dark mode

The platform fully supports dark mode. All buttons, text, and interface elements remain clearly readable in both light and dark themes. Dark mode follows your device or browser's system preference automatically — there is no manual toggle needed.

## 9. Getting Help

### If something is not working

Try these steps first:

1. **Refresh the page** — most loading issues resolve with a simple refresh.
2. **Sign out and sign back in** — if pages are not loading correctly, this resets your session.
3. **Check your internet connection** — the platform requires an active internet connection throughout your session.
4. **Try a different browser** — the platform works best on up-to-date versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari.

### If you cannot log in

- Check that you are using the email address your administrator registered for you.
- Use the **Forgot password** link to reset your password if needed.
- If you are trying to use Single Sign-On and it is not working, contact your organisation administrator — they will need to confirm your account is set up correctly.

### If you have a question about the training content

The training content is reviewed by specialists. If something in a lesson seems unclear, note your question and raise it with your line manager or the professional you work alongside. They will be best placed to help you apply the learning in your specific context.

### The built-in How to Guide

The platform has an in-built guide available at any time. Click **How to Guide** in the left-hand sidebar. This guide covers the same ground as this document and is always up to date.

### Contacting your organisation administrator

For account issues — password resets, access problems, or questions about which training has been assigned to you — your first point of contact is your organisation's administrator, not the platform support team.
