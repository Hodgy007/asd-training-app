## 1. Welcome

Welcome to the Ambitious about Autism training platform. This guide is written for careers leaders, SENCOs, SEND co-ordinators, and anyone in a guidance or pastoral role who supports autistic young people into education, training, or employment.

As a **Careers Professional**, you have access to a range of tools designed to support both your own continuing professional development and the practical work you do with the young people in your care. Alongside structured CPD training modules, the platform provides curated job listings and student management tools — all in one place.

**What this platform is not:** The training content does not lead to a formal qualification or accreditation. It is a structured, evidence-informed CPD resource to support and document your professional learning, aligned with the Gatsby Benchmarks framework for careers education.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Your account

Self-registration is not available on this platform. Your account is created by your organisation's administrator, who will send you your login credentials directly. There is no "Sign up" or "Register" button on the platform.

If you have not received your credentials, contact your organisation administrator or the person who arranged your platform access.

### 2.2 Signing in

Navigate to the platform at [https://asd-training-app-v2.vercel.app](https://asd-training-app-v2.vercel.app).

The login page auto-detects the sign-in method from your email — no toggle to flip.

**Email & Password**
Enter the email address and password provided by your administrator (or chosen via the welcome email if you self-registered), then select **Sign in**. You will be prompted to change your password on first sign-in if your administrator set a temporary one for you. If you forget your password at any time, select **Forgot password?** on the login page and a reset link will be sent to your email inbox.

**Single Sign-On (corporate IdP)**
If your organisation has configured enterprise SSO, a **Sign in with [your organisation]** button appears once you type your email. Click it to sign in through your usual corporate identity provider.

**Google or Microsoft**
If the charity has enabled Google or Microsoft sign-in, those buttons appear inline above the password form. If you sign in this way and don't have an account yet, you will be taken to a brief one-question registration page to finish setting up. No "access denied" — first-time OAuth users can self-register.

**Self-registering as a Careers Professional**
If your organisation already exists on the platform but you don't have an account, you can register yourself at `/register`. Choose **I work for or study at an existing organisation**, find your organisation in the list, and select the **Careers Professional** role. You will receive a welcome email with a link to set your password.

### 2.3 Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA is not mandatory for the Careers Professional role. You will not be required to configure a TOTP authenticator app.

### 2.4 Your sidebar

Once signed in, the sidebar on the left provides your main navigation. The links you see depend on the training programmes and document collections your organisation has enabled. A typical sidebar for a Careers Professional looks like this:

| Link | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your home screen |
| **[Training programme name(s)]** | Your assigned training programmes (e.g. Careers CPD Training) |
| **[Document collection links]** | Any document collections shared with your role |
| **How to Guide** | This guide, accessible in-platform |
| **Jobs** | Curated job listings for students |
| **My Students** | Manage your students and view their progress |
| **Workshops** | Upcoming and past virtual classroom sessions |
| **Settings** | Password and account settings (bottom of sidebar) |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out of the platform (bottom of sidebar) |

If your organisation has granted you access to additional training programmes, each will appear as a separate link in the sidebar.

## 3. Your Dashboard

After signing in you are taken to your **Dashboard** at `/dashboard`. This is your home screen and gives you a quick overview of your activity and upcoming commitments.

### 3.1 Training progress

The dashboard shows your completion across all assigned training programmes — total lessons completed out of the total available, and a per-programme progress card with a **Start** or **Continue** link.

### 3.2 Upcoming workshops

If you have been added as an attendee or host for an upcoming virtual classroom session, an **Upcoming Sessions** card will appear on your dashboard showing the next session's title, date, and time.

### 3.3 Announcements

If your organisation's administrator or the platform team has posted an announcement — such as information about new content, events, or policy updates — it will appear in a highlighted banner near the top of your dashboard. Announcements may have an expiry date and will disappear automatically once they are no longer active.

### 3.4 Feature tiles

Depending on your organisation's settings, you may see a quick-access tile for the Jobs feature.

## 4. Careers Training

Select the training programme link in the sidebar to reach the training hub for that programme.

### 4.1 Overview page

At the top of the training hub you will see a progress card showing your overall completion — total lessons completed, total available, and an overall percentage. Below this are cards for each module within the programme.

### 4.2 Working through a module

Select **Start module** or **Continue** on a module card to open it. Locked modules show a padlock icon — they will unlock once the preceding module is fully complete.

On the **module overview page** you will see:

- The module description and your current progress bar.
- A **Start first lesson** or **Continue learning** button that takes you straight to your next incomplete lesson.
- A full list of all lessons in the module, each showing its type (text, video, or SCORM) and the number of quiz questions it contains. Completed lessons are marked with a green tick. Within a module, lessons are completed in sequence — a lesson unlocks only after the previous one is finished.

### 4.3 Lesson features

Each lesson page may include a combination of the following:

**Text-to-speech (TTS)**
A play button above the lesson title reads the lesson content aloud using a clear British female voice. For lessons with carousel blocks, each slide has its own TTS play button. For prose-only lessons, a player also appears at the bottom of the page. MP3s are pre-generated so playback starts immediately.

**Interactive blocks**
Lessons may include:
- *Carousels* — slide-by-slide content with per-slide TTS
- *Hotspot images* — click on areas of an image to reveal more information
- *Video embeds* — embedded videos within the lesson flow
- *Rich-text sections* — formatted prose with headings, lists, and images

**Quiz**
Most lessons end with a knowledge check quiz. Answers are multiple-choice. After submitting each answer you receive immediate feedback and a brief explanation. You must complete all quiz questions to mark the lesson as finished.

**Certificate of Completion**
When you finish a lesson, a Certificate of Completion is displayed featuring the Ambitious about Autism logo. This can be printed or saved as evidence of CPD.

### 4.4 Progress saving

Progress is saved automatically when you complete a quiz. If you close the browser or navigate away before finishing the quiz, the lesson will not be marked as complete and you can return to it at any time. Completed lessons remain complete permanently.

### 4.5 Reviewing completed lessons

Completed lessons remain fully accessible. On the module overview page, select **Review** next to any completed lesson to re-read its content at any time.

## 5. Job Openings

The Jobs feature surfaces charity-curated job opportunities to students, interns, and employees. Select **Jobs** in the sidebar to reach it at `/jobs`.

### 5.1 Browsing job listings

The Jobs page shows all published job openings that are targeted to your organisation or role. Each listing shows:

- Employer name and details
- Job title and description
- Employment type: Internship, Apprenticeship, Part-time, Full-time, or Volunteer
- Location type: On-site, Hybrid, or Remote
- Closing date
- Autism-friendly notes (where provided by the employer)

Select a listing to open the full job detail view.

### 5.2 Job assignments

The charity admin can assign specific job listings to individual students via your CDO account. If a job has been assigned to a particular student, it will appear in that student's job list. This is managed by the charity team — if you need a specific job highlighted for a student, contact your platform administrator.

## 6. My Students

The **My Students** section lets you manage the STUDENT, INTERN, and EMPLOYEE users within your organisation. Select **My Students** in the sidebar.

### 6.1 Your student list

You will see a list of all students, interns, and employees in your organisation. From here you can:

- View each student's profile and contact details.
- See their training progress across assigned programmes.

### 6.2 Training progress

Select a student's name to open their profile. Their training progress shows completion per programme and per lesson, so you can identify who may need a nudge or additional support.

## 7. Workshops and Sessions

Your organisation can run live virtual classroom sessions over Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or a custom link. Select **Workshops** in the sidebar to view your sessions at `/sessions`.

### 7.1 Viewing your sessions

The Sessions page shows all sessions you have been invited to, with their status: Upcoming, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled.

### 7.2 Joining a session

When a session is ready, a **Join** button will appear. Selecting it opens the meeting link in a new tab.

### 7.3 Upcoming sessions on the dashboard

When you have an upcoming session, a card will appear on your Dashboard showing the title, date, and time at a glance.

### 7.4 Recordings

After a session has taken place, your organisation's admin may add a recording link. If one is available, a **Watch recording** link will appear on that session.

### 7.5 Hosting a session

If your administrator has assigned you as the **host** of a session, you will have additional options on that session's page:

- **Meeting link** — add or update the link that attendees will use to join.
- **Attendance** — mark which attendees were present during or after the session.
- **Recording** — add a recording URL after the session ends so attendees can watch it back.

## 8. Settings

Select **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar to reach your account settings at `/settings`.

### 8.1 Changing your password

The Settings page lets you change your password at any time. Enter your current password and your new password, then confirm. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.

### 8.2 Dark mode

The platform follows your system preference for dark mode automatically. All interface elements — including role badges, progress indicators, and form controls — are clearly readable in both light and dark themes.

## 9. Getting Help

**How to Guide**
An in-platform guide is available at `/guide`. Select **How to Guide** in the sidebar to access it at any time.

**Technical issues**
If you experience login problems, pages not loading, or progress not saving, contact your organisation administrator in the first instance. For platform-wide technical issues, email **support@ambitiousaboutautism.org.uk** with a description of the issue and the browser you are using.

**Content queries**
For questions about module content or the Gatsby Benchmarks framework, speak to your line manager or SEND lead, or refer to the [Gatsby Foundation careers guidance resources](https://www.gatsby.org.uk/education/focus-areas/good-career-guidance).

**Account administration**
For role changes, adding colleagues, or organisational access queries, contact your organisation's platform administrator. Administrators manage user accounts through the admin panel and can create new accounts, reset passwords, and adjust access.

**Privacy and data requests**
Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, and request erasure of your personal data. To make a request, email **privacy@ambitiousaboutautism.org.uk** with your name and the nature of your request.
